Biography

I am currently an undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science and Technology at Northeastern University. During my undergraduate studies, I conducted a research internship at Tsinghua University. My work has focused on Visual In-Context Learning, Multimodal Large Language Models, and Visual Abstract Reasoning, with an emphasis on leveraging in-context examples to improve task adaptation and strengthening models' understanding and reasoning over visual structures and abstract rules.

During my doctoral studies, I plan to further explore Embodied AI and World Models, focusing on how agents learn physical properties and dynamics through environmental interaction and transfer acquired knowledge across objects and tasks to improve generalization and autonomous decision-making in open-world environments.

Research Interests

My research interests center on Spatial Intelligence, Embodied Intelligence, and AI Agents. I study how intelligent systems understand and reason about 3D space, scene structure, and physical relations; how agents learn perception-planning-action loops in real or simulated environments; and how multimodal agents plan tasks, use tools, interact with environments, and act autonomously over long horizons.

LongVideoClaw

Interactive Multi-Agent System for Long-Video Understanding

GitHub

LongVideoClaw implements a multi-agent loop for planning, localization, reasoning, verification, and revision. A hierarchical Super / Macro / Micro Event memory graph identifies key segments in long videos, while frame-level visual reasoning and a Visual Critic enable evidence-grounded, traceable, and self-correcting video question answering.

Research Experience

Label-Aware Prompt Retrieval for Visual In-Context Learning (LaPR)
2025.08 — 2025.11
Tsinghua University

To address performance degradation caused by prompt retrieval that overlooks label consistency in visual in-context learning (VICL), I led the development of the label-aware prompt retrieval framework LaPR:

  • Designed a joint image-label representation that incorporates visual label information into in-context prompts.
  • Introduced mixture-of-experts (MoE) modules with query-adaptive routing in both prompt and query encoders to maintain label consistency.
  • Achieved state-of-the-art results on foreground segmentation, object detection, and image colorization.
  • Published at CVPR 2026 as a co-first author.
Visual Abstract Reasoning with Visual In-Context Learning (MIRA)
2025.12 — 2026.07
Tsinghua University

To address the difficulty of encoding complex transformation rules with a single task representation in ARC, I proposed and developed MIRA, a visual-context-based analogical reasoning framework:

  • Constructed explicit context sequences from demonstrations and query images as model inputs.
  • Introduced a context adapter that extracts input-output transformation rules through pairwise self-attention over demonstrations.
  • Applied the extracted rules dynamically to query images through cross-attention for context-driven analogical reasoning.
  • Led the project, proposed the MIRA framework, and completed the experimental implementation and paper writing.
Full-Duplex Speech Dialogue Model Evaluation Benchmark (FLEXI)

NEUNLPLab
